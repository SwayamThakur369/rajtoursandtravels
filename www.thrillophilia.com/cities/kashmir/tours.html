What Makes Kashmir Holiday Package Prices Go Up or Down

A Kashmir trip cost mainly depends on four factors:

1. Travel Season: Prices are highest during the summer season (April–June) and winter snowfall season (December–February), when hotel and flight rates increase due to high demand.

2. Local Transportation: Some attractions, such as Betaab Valley and Thajiwas Glacier, require local taxis that are often not included in standard packages, adding to the overall cost.

3. Gondola Tickets: Gulmarg Gondola tickets, especially for Phase 2, are in high demand and can increase trip costs if booked separately.

4. Houseboat Stays: The type and location of your houseboat matter. Premium houseboats, particularly on Nigeen Lake, cost more than standard options on Dal Lake.

How to Reach Kashmir

Kashmir is one of India's most visited domestic destinations. One of the main reasons for this popularity is its good connectivity through flights, trains, and roads, making it accessible from major cities across India.

By Air

Flying is the fastest and most convenient way to reach Kashmir, making it the preferred choice for most travellers. Srinagar Airport is well-connected to major Indian cities, with direct flights available from Delhi, Mumbai, and Chandigarh. Travellers from cities such as Kolkata, Bengaluru, and Hyderabad mostly connect through Delhi. Direct flights from Delhi are often the most economical option, while connecting flights via Delhi can sometimes cost INR 1,500 to INR 3,000 less than premium non-stop alternatives on certain routes. Based on airfare trends, flights departing on Thursday evenings and Friday mornings generally experience a 20–35% price increase due to higher demand.

By Train

Travelling by train is a budget-friendly option, particularly for those who enjoy scenic journeys. While Srinagar does not currently have a direct railway connection, Jammu Tawi Railway Station serves as the nearest major railhead and is well-connected to cities across India. Popular services include the New Delhi–Jammu Tawi Rajdhani Express and the Jhelum Express. From Jammu, travellers continue their journey to Srinagar by road, with the drive taking around 5–6 hours depending on traffic and road conditions.

By Road

A road trip to Kashmir offers a memorable experience for travellers who enjoy mountain landscapes and scenic drives. The journey primarily follows National Highway 44 (NH-44), passing notable landmarks such as the Jawahar Tunnel and Chenani-Nashri Tunnel. Regular transportation options include private taxis as well as JKSRTC government buses operating between Jammu and Srinagar. Travellers driving from Delhi cover approximately 800 kilometres, with the journey usually taking 18–20 hours depending on traffic, weather conditions, and road status.

Best Places to Visit in Kashmir

Kashmir is home to peaceful lakes, green valleys, snow-covered mountains, and scenic hill towns. Here are the best places to visit on your next Kashmir Trip

Srinagar

Srinagar is the heart of Kashmir and the starting point for most trips. Known for its beautiful lakes, Mughal gardens, and traditional houseboats, the city offers a perfect introduction to the region. A Shikara ride on Dal Lake, a stroll along Boulevard Road, and a visit to Nishat Bagh and Shalimar Bagh are some of the most popular things to do here.

What to Know

  • Ideal stay: 1.5–2 days
  • Best place to start and end your Kashmir trip
  • Houseboat location often matters more than the category of houseboat

Pahalgam

Located along the Lidder River, Pahalgam is known for its peaceful atmosphere and beautiful natural surroundings. The town serves as a gateway to attractions like Betaab Valley and Aru Valley, while also offering activities such as river rafting, horse riding, trekking, nature walks, and sightseeing. Its relaxed pace and scenic landscapes make it a great destination for travellers looking to slow down and enjoy nature.

What to Know

  • Ideal stay: 1–2 nights
  • Best for relaxation and scenic exploration
  • Aru Valley is usually less crowded than other nearby attractions

Gulmarg

Gulmarg is Kashmir’s most famous mountain destination and is known for its stunning landscapes throughout the year. During winter, it becomes one of India's top skiing destinations, while summer brings lush meadows and panoramic mountain views. The Gulmarg Gondola is the town’s main attraction, while activities such as skiing, sledging, horse riding, ATV rides, and sightseeing make it one of Kashmir’s most popular destinations year-round.

What to Know

  • Ideal stay: 1–2 nights
  • Popular in both summer and winter seasons
  • Home to one of the highest cable car rides in the world

Sonmarg

Sonmarg, often called the "Meadow of Gold", is known for its scenic mountain landscapes, glaciers, and fast-flowing rivers. Compared to other tourist destinations in Kashmir, it feels more remote and less commercialised, making it a favourite among nature lovers. Visitors can enjoy activities such as pony rides to nearby viewpoints, glacier excursions, short hikes, camping, trout fishing, and photography amidst some of Kashmir's most dramatic scenery.

What to Know

  • Best suited for longer Kashmir itineraries
  • Road accessibility can depend on weather conditions
  • Ideal for travellers seeking less-crowded natural landscapes

Best Time to Visit Kashmir: Season-by-Season Guide

Kashmir experiences four distinct seasons, and each transforms the landscape in a completely different way. From snow-covered mountains in winter to blooming tulip gardens in spring and golden Chinar trees in autumn, the best time to visit Kashmir depends on the experience you're looking for.

Winter (December to February)

Winter is the best time to visit Kashmir if you want to experience snowfall. The valley looks completely different during these months, with snow-covered mountains, frozen lakes, and chilly weather. Gulmarg becomes a favourite spot for skiing, snowboarding, and other snow activities, while Srinagar offers beautiful winter views with occasional snowfall and misty mornings. Temperatures usually range from -4°C to 10°C. Since heavy snowfall can sometimes affect road travel, it is helpful to keep some flexibility in your plans.

Spring (March to May)

Spring brings Kashmir back to life after winter. Gardens and valleys fill with colourful flowers, almond blossoms begin to bloom, and the famous Tulip Garden in Srinagar becomes one of the region's biggest attractions. Snow can still be seen on the surrounding mountains, creating a beautiful contrast with the fresh greenery below. With temperatures ranging between 8°C and 20°C, the weather is comfortable for sightseeing, photography, and outdoor activities. This is one of the most popular seasons for visitors.

Summer (June to August)

Summer is considered one of the best times to visit Kashmir. The weather remains pleasant, the skies are usually clear, and all major tourist destinations are easily accessible. Temperatures generally range from 15°C to 30°C, making it comfortable for sightseeing and outdoor experiences. Whether it is a Shikara ride on Dal Lake, a visit to the meadows of Gulmarg, or exploring the valleys of Pahalgam and Sonmarg, summer offers ideal conditions for a relaxed holiday. It is also the most popular season for family vacations.

Autumn (September to November)

Autumn gives Kashmir a completely different charm. The famous Chinar trees turn into shades of yellow, orange, and red, creating some of the most beautiful landscapes in the valley. The weather remains pleasant, with temperatures usually ranging from 7°C to 22°C, making it ideal for sightseeing and photography. Since there are fewer tourists compared to the summer season, travellers can enjoy a quieter and more peaceful experience while admiring the region's colourful scenery.

Best Dishes to Try in Kashmir

Kashmir is known for its rich and flavourful cuisine. While many traditional dishes are meat-based, vegetarian travellers can also enjoy plenty of delicious local specialities. Most hotels and restaurants can arrange vegetarian meals if informed in advance.

Wazwan: Kashmir's Traditional Feast

Wazwan is a famous multi-course Kashmiri meal prepared by expert chefs known as wazas. It is served with rice and includes several traditional dishes such as Rogan Josh, Yakhni, Gushtaba, and Tabak Maaz. Wazwan is known for its rich flavours and generous portions.

Other Popular Vegetarian Dishes

  • Haak Saag – A simple and healthy Kashmiri leafy green dish.
  • Nadru Yakhni – Lotus stem cooked in a creamy yogurt-based gravy.
  • Dum Aloo – Potatoes cooked in a flavorful Kashmiri spice blend.
  • Rajma Chawal – Kidney beans served with rice, a local favourite.

Must-Try Kashmiri Specialties

  • Gushtaba – Soft mutton meatballs slow-cooked in a rich yogurt gravy.
  • Tabak Maaz – Crispy fried lamb ribs, tender on the inside and crunchy outside.
  • Lyodur Tschaman (Waza Paneer) – Cottage cheese cooked in a mild yogurt and turmeric-based sauce.
  • Masala Tsot – A popular street-food wrap made with Lavasa bread, spiced white peas, and tangy radish-yoghurt chutney.

Where to Try These Dishes in Kashmir

Many of these traditional Kashmiri dishes can be enjoyed at popular restaurants in Srinagar, such as Ahdoos, Mughal Darbar, and Shamyana Restaurant, all known for serving authentic local cuisine. Visitors can also find regional specialties at local eateries around Dal Lake, Lal Chowk, and Residency Road. For a more immersive experience, several heritage hotels and resorts in Srinagar, Gulmarg, and Pahalgam offer traditional Kashmiri meals, including Wazwan. Street-food favourites like Masala Tsot are best sampled from local bakeries and food stalls in Srinagar's bustling markets.

Kashmir Travel Essentials & Safety Advisory for Indian Travellers

Kashmir is one of those destinations where a well-planned trip makes all the difference. For most travellers visiting popular places like Srinagar, Gulmarg, and Pahalgam in 2026, the experience is safe, comfortable, and well-managed.

According to a January 2026 WION report, Kashmir saw a significant increase in winter tourist arrivals, especially in Srinagar and Gulmarg. To manage the growing number of visitors, authorities have strengthened security arrangements and improved crowd management during peak travel periods.

Plan Your Gulmarg Gondola Ride Early: The Gulmarg Gondola is one of the most popular experiences in Kashmir, but it requires some planning. During peak winter months, especially December and January, queues at the gondola counters start getting longer by around 10 AM. Arriving early can help you avoid long waits and save 1–2 hours.

Houseboat Stays Offer a Unique Experience: Whether or not to stay in a houseboat depends on your travel style, but many visitors consider it one of the highlights of their Kashmir trip. Staying on a houseboat on Dal Lake offers a unique experience that feels very different from a regular hotel stay. Luxury houseboats are also available for travellers looking for added comfort and premium facilities.

Hotels Provide Comfort and Convenience: Hotels in Srinagar are ideal for travellers who prefer modern comforts and easy access to the city. They generally offer better heating facilities during winter and are conveniently located near major attractions, shopping areas, and restaurants.
