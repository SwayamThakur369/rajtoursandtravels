Popular Destinations to Visit on a Himachal Tour:

1. Shimla:

Nestled on the foothills of the Himalayas, Shimla offers the perfect summer getaway. Now the capital city of the state, Shimla served as the erstwhile summer capital in the colonial eras, the influence of which still prevails in the local architecture. The bustling Mall Road runs through the heart of the city, dotted with departmental stores, retail outlets. Running parallels is the famed Shimla Ridge, a scenic walkway featuring a panoramic view of the Himalayas. The Neolithic Christ Church stands at one end, a testament to the city’s colonial past. A short drive away lies Annandale, Shimla’s biggest grassy glade featuring horse rides and a golf course. This is a must-visit spot to be included in your Shimla Tour Packages.

2. Manali:

Manali is a picturesque town situated in the Beas River valley, renowned for its blend of nature and culture. Manali is a popular destination in Manali tour packages, ideal for couples and families. The Beas River flows through the town, creating a natural boundary with Old Manali, a charming area filled with quaint cafes and local art shops. Amidst the forests of Old Manali stands the ancient Hadimba Temple, celebrated for its unique architecture.

3. Dharamshala:

Dharamshala is the winter capital known for its stunning beauty. With its sweeping landscapes, it offers a peaceful getaway in the hills. You'll find many Hindu temples scattered across the mountains, like the Indru Nag Temple, Chamunda Devi Temple, and Kunal Pathri Temple. St. John’s Church in the Wilderness is also noteworthy for its beautiful Belgian Glass Window. Just a short distance away lies Mcleodganj, a charming Tibetan town that adds to Dharamshala's charm. From there, you can venture into Dharamkot, a popular spot for trekking in the northeast. This is one of the most recommended places to include in your Dharamshala tour packages.

4. Dalhousie:

Dalhousie is a popular place to visit in himachal-pradesh. It's made up of five hills and was started by Lord Dalhousie in 1850. It's called a hill station because it's high up in the mountains and is a cool place to escape the summer heat. The Dhauladhar mountains make a beautiful backdrop. You'll see wooden houses and waterfalls everywhere. Two famous waterfalls are Satdhara and Panjpulla. People believe Panjpulla has healing powers. There are also old churches from the time when the British ruled here. For those planning a visit to this enchanting destination, Dalhousie tour packages promise an immersive experience into the scenic beauty, historical charm, and serene ambiance of this Himalayan retreat. 

5. Bir Billing:

Commonly dubbed as the ‘Paragliding capital of India’, Bir Billing is a must-have inclusion in all Himachal tour packages designed for adventure lovers. The little village sits idly in the Joginder Nagar Valley of Himachal. The sweeping landscape and ideal weather of Bir Billing make it perfect for paragliding, and the region is often selected to host the World Cup in the sport. Additionally, Bir Billing is regarded as a popular meditation center and has several spiritual retreats catering to Buddhist practices. A few gorgeous trekking trails snake their way through the town as well. For adventure seekers, Bir Billing tour packages promise an adrenaline-pumping experience amidst the breathtaking vistas of the Himalayas.

6. Kasauli:

Home to gabled cottage houses and expensive orchids that bloom every spring, Kasauli embodies the quaint colonial charm that the Himachali cities are so well known for. The best way to explore this hill station is to take one of its many cobbled lanes and explore the beauty of the terrain on foot. The highest point in Kasauli is the Monkey Point or Manki Point as the locals call it, commanding unparalleled views of the surrounding peaks. The hill station is also home to the famed Krishna Bhavan Mandir, a Hindu temple designed after a Church and treated as an architectural marvel. For an enriching experience that combines colonial charm, natural beauty, and cultural marvels, Kasauli tour packages are the key to unlocking the secrets of this Himalayan gem. Explore

7. Mcleodganj:

A spiritual retreat in the heart of the hills, the little hamlet of Mcleodganj is well known for its serenity. Owing to its considerable Tibetan populace, the Mcleodganj is often dubbed as the “Little Lhasa ''. The stunning Kareri Lake at the center of the town is one of the primary attractions here. The nearby Minkani pass, apart from its hiking routes, is also famed for its lush flowering meadows. Mcleodganj is a popular hub for trekking enthusiasts as well, making it one of the top places in himachal-pradesh packages for the sport. Several popular trekking routes cut their way across the hills here, the Triund trek being one of the most popular names, making it a popular trekking destination in himachal-pradesh. For a holistic experience that combines spirituality, natural beauty, and adventure, Mcleodganj tour packages open the door to a world of serenity and exploration.

Best Time to Go For a himachal-pradesh Trip:

himachal-pradesh can be visited all round the year as it has something unique to offer every season. December to February is ideal for adventure lovers as they can enjoy various adventure activities such as snowboarding and skiing. March to April and October to November are the months of calm, as winter arrives and leaves, perfect to enjoy a serene holiday. The months of May to September provide the visitors with amazing weather which is suitable to undertake sightseeing and hiking. Overall, the best time to visit himachal-pradesh depends on your preferences and the experiences you seek.

How to Reach himachal-pradesh:

1. By Air:

himachal-pradesh is serviced by three main airports- Jubal Hatti, Bhuntar, and Kangra. The Jubal Hatti airport is the best option for those traveling to Shimla or Manali in their himachal-pradesh tour packages, while Kullu and the other surrounding regions are easily accessed via Bhuntar. Kangra airport is the closest to Dharamshala, and one can also travel to Mcleodganj, Dalhousie, or Khajjiar from here. Most flights are largely limited to Delhi, so one would first need to arrive at Delhi before taking a connecting flight.

2. By Rail:

There are two ways to reach Himachal by train. A major broad gauge station connects Pathankot to Jogindernagar in Kangra Valley. Alternatively, a colonial-era toy train connects Kalka to Shimla.

3. By Road:

The best way to reach Himachal is by road. The towns here are well connected to the major surrounding city via public transport. Interstate bus services can be availed from Delhi, Chandigarh, Jaipur, or any of the other neighboring cities in the region. Some roads may be blocked or inaccessible during heavy monsoon showers and should be inquired about beforehand booking Himachal tour packages.

Places to Visit on a Himachal Tour:

Here are some of the best places to visit on a Himachal tour:

1. Hadimba Temple, Manali:

Nestled in the snowy hills of Manali, Hadimba Temple is a unique shrine dedicated to Hidimba Devi. Surrounded by cedar forests, the temple stands on a rock resembling the goddess herself. Known as Dhungari Temple, its wooden structure sets it apart from the other destinations included in our himachal-pradesh packages. 

2. Mall Road, Shimla:

It is a bustling commercial hub that symbolises the spirit of the town. Lined with restaurants, shops, and theatres, it is an ideal place for people who love strolling down the streets of a lively market. Renowned for woollen goods, wooden crafts, and cafes, it offers a delightful shopping experience. The Mall Road in Shimla is not just a street; it's a vibrant tapestry of culture and commerce, drawing locals and tourists alike to its charming ambiance.

3. Triund Hill, Dharamshala:

Crowned as the ‘Jewel of Dharamshala’, Triund Hill offers breathtaking views amidst the Dhauladhar mountains. It is a popular trekking spot that features a rocky climb through stunning oak and deodar forests. Upon visiting, you will discover the picturesque top, perfect for a camping spot.

4. Satdhara Falls, Dalhousie:

Included in the Himachal travel packages, Satdhara Falls in Dalhousie is an iconic waterfall where seven springs meet. It offers tranquillity, with views of Chamba Valley and its medicinal mica-infused spring waters that cascades into an aquamarine pool surrounded by a meadow of blooming flowers.

5. Meditation Centers, Bir Billing:

Meditation Centers in Bir Billing is another place that you must visit in himachal-pradesh if you are looking for solitude. It attracts a myriad of tourists from different parts of the world to learn about and practise the different kinds of meditations. This place of retreat is popular amongst the spiritual travellers as well.

6. Monkey Point, Kasauli:

It is the highest spot in the town that provides stunning panoramic views. It features a temple dedicated to Lord Hanuman, who is believed to have touched the spot while fetching the Sanjeevani herb. The Monkey Point in Kasauli offers a bird’s eye view of Chandigarh, Kalka, and the Sutlej River. 

7. Kareri Lake, McLeodganj:

Situated at 2,934 metres, Kareri Lake is a picturesque dream-like destination in McLeodganj. Fed by melting snow from the Dhauladhar range, the pristine waters of the lake flow into the Nyund stream. It is a popular trekking destination in our himachal-pradesh packages as it features lush pasturelands and vibrant flowers surrounding a magnificent lake. 

Things to Do on a Himachal Trip:

Here are some of the best things to do on a Himachal trip:

1. Paragliding at Bir:

Located at a height of over 8,000 feet above sea level, Bir Billing is an absolute must-have in all himachal-pradesh tour packages for adventure lovers. Paragliding in Bir takes place between the months of June and September when the weather here remains at its best. The sport is completely guided, making it ideal for first-time fliers as well. After a training session at the launchpad, fliers take off into the sky, witnessing unprecedented views of the Dhauladhar. Each flight lasts 20 minutes and can be video recorded on request

2. Rafting in Kullu Valley:

Cutting its way through Kullu Valley, the river Beas offers the perfect locale for rafting. One of the best inclusions in Himachal packages for those with a penchant for water sports, this group activity is completely guided and can be attempted by all. The rapids here range from Grade 1-3, offering a perfect learning experience for first-time rafters. The experience lasts between 30 minutes to an hour, depending on the rafters’ wishes. Water activities in Kullu Valley are a must include in Himachal tour packages.

3. Trek to Malana:

Nestled underneath the Chanderkhani Pass in Manali is the little village of Malana. Characterized by its little wooden houses, the rustic beauty of Malana attracts trekkers to itself throughout summer. Commanding excellent views of the surrounding mountains, the Malana trek is sure to please the aesthete in you, making it a popular inclusion in Himachal tour packages. The trekking routes also cross some major tourist destinations here, such as the Hanuman Tibba and the Chanderkhani Pass. The trek only takes place between June and September, when the weather conditions are ideal

4. Shopping from Mall Road in Shimla:

When in Shimla, one cannot afford to miss out on the chance to go on a shopping spree at Mall Road. With its streets lined up with independent sellers and retail stores, shopping at the Mall can be quite the experience that you can get in Himachal packages. A variety of items can be purchased here, ranging from books to clothes and even accessories. The road is lined up with several quaint cafes as well.

5. Skiing at Solang Valley:

Although summers in the valley can be equally as rewarding, winter transforms Solang into a haven for snow sports. With its slopes covered in pristine blankets of snow, the terrain of Solang Valley in winter becomes an ideal and popular hub for skiing enthusiasts making it a great place to include in Himachal trip packages. While experts in the sport can easily take their own skis and navigate the slopes on their own accord, first-timers can avail the expert guidance of any of the trainers here. Skiing in Solang is best experienced during the winter months, between October and January.

6. Wildlife Tours and Safari:

The hill stations of Himachal have attracted hundreds and thousands of tourists for its pristine landscape and natural backdrop. Many Himachal packages offer wildlife tours and safaris through the well populated and lush green forests of Oak, Deodar, Blue Pine, Fir, Adlers, Birch and Spruce covering the state. Housing over 1500 birds and 320 species of animals, it's 32 wildlife sanctuaries and 2 national parks are great destinations for nature and wildlife lovers. Explore and experience the serene atmosphere, as you trek through or take a Jeep Safari in the natural scenery of Chail Wildlife Sanctuary or Himalayan National Park.

7. Rock Climbing in Vashisht:

Manali is a famous destination in Himachal Tour Packages, both for a backpacking trip with friends or romantic tour with a loved one. In this beautiful location rock climbing is one of the most popular activities that tickles the adventure bone of tourists coming to himachal-pradesh. Set in the backdrop of the charming city of Manali, Rock Climbing in Vashisht gives a breathtaking view of the surroundings as you experience the thrill of rare climbing spots in the Kullu Valley. You are harnessed safely as you climb the rocks, and a cold breeze caresses your face. This one of a lifetime kind of experience should definitely be on your list while visiting Himachal. 

8. Mountain Biking in Spiti:

Mountain Biking amidst the majestic peaks and awe inspiring backgrounds of the Himalayas is an irreplaceable and thrilling experience. A comparatively safe adventure activity for those who wish to explore the Himachal terrains, biking amongst the wonderful rock formations of the valley is popular in Himachal Tours. The experience encourages you to push your limits as you cycle through different trails and witness the magnificent beauty of the Himalayas. 

9. Jeep Safari In Himachal:

A fun adventurous experience, Jeep Safari in Himachal takes you across the remote destinations and offbeat tourist spots. Offering you the panoramic view of the peaks of Himalayas, taking you to camping through the picturesque valleys and providing you with the real essence of the Himalayan ranges. A Jeep Safari trip is the perfect way to explore Himachal by travel lovers, as you will be exploring the locales and getting in touch with the natives personally. You can also select a Jeep Safari that will take you in the deepest and most offbeat parts of the valleys. 

10. Ice Skating:

The clear skies, snow laden lands and cold breeze - Ice skating in himachal-pradesh is a great experience for people of all ages. The state capital, Shimla is the best destination for enjoying or trying ice skating, closely followed by Ice skating in Narkanda, Manali and Rohtang Pass. Every year, in the month of December you can find yourself in the midst of a grand winter festival in Shimla. Many private tourism companies, and adventure sport Clubs offer this activity to tourists in winters. If you are looking forward to trying something new and exhilarating to do when in Himachal during winters, keep ice skating at the top of your list.

Must Try Local Dishes in himachal-pradesh :

If you are planning to book Himachal tour packages then here is a list of popular dishes that you must try during your trip.

1 Siddu:

Siddu is one of the most loved traditional dishes of himachal-pradesh by tourists. It is a steamed dish made of wheat flour and includes vegetarian as well as non-vegetarian fillings. It is easily available in restaurants, cafes and food stalls on the streets. 

2 Chha Gosht:

Chha Gosht is a one-pot dish belonging to the Gaddi tribe of himachal-pradesh. It is a popular dish among non-veg lovers and includes mutton, yoghurt, gram flour and spices like chilli powder, asafoetida, ginger, garlic, coriander powder, bay leaf and cardamom.

3 Tudkiya Bhath:

Tudkiya Bhath is a rice recipe from himachal-pradesh. It requires freshly ground spices along with yoghurt, onion, garlic, ginger, potato, tomato, cardamom and lentils. 

4 Dham:

Another delicious pulao recipe is Dham which is mainly made on occasions like marriage, anniversaries and birthdays. It includes aromatic rice, several pulses, chickpeas, lentils, vegetables and kidney beans. The base of the dish is made with tamarind and jaggery and is mainly served with a side dish.

5 Madra:

Madra is a popular side dish which is served with flavoured rice and chapatis. The main ingredients of this dish are chickpeas, kidney beans and black-eyed beans. The base or the curry is made of curd and legume which is mixed with several spices.

Which are the beautiful lakes to visit in Himachal?

himachal-pradesh is home to several stunning lakes that offer serene and picturesque surroundings. Some of the beautiful lakes to visit in himachal-pradesh with the Himachal tours include:

1. Chandratal Lake: Located in the Spiti Valley, this high-altitude lake is known for its breathtaking beauty and crystal-clear waters.

2. Prashar Lake: Situated in the Mandi district, this enchanting lake is surrounded by snow-capped peaks and offers panoramic views of the Himalayas.

3. Khajjiar Lake: Known as the "Mini Switzerland of India," this picturesque lake in Khajjiar is surrounded by meadows and dense forests, making it a popular tourist spot.

4. Renuka Lake: One of the largest lakes in Sirmaur District of himachal-pradesh. Surrounded by hills and a wildlife sanctuary, it is a serene destination for nature lovers.

5. Manimahesh Lake: This sacred lake in the Chamba district is believed to be the abode of Lord Shiva. Visit this astounding lake with himachal-pradesh tour packages which is surrounded by snow-clad peaks and is also a popular pilgrimage site.

Which are some famous treks to do in himachal-pradesh?

1. Hampta Pass Trek: Hampta Pass is named after the beautiful Hampta Village lying in the mountainous region of Pir Panjal. Perched at an elevation of around 13,500 feet, the popular Hampta Pass is an amazing destination to trek to. Surrounded by lush meadows, jagged mountains, shimmering rivulets, and beautiful glaciers, the trek offers easy to moderate trails that require at least 5 days to be traversed. Although this trek can be done by the novice trekkers, they need to be physically fit and confident for the same.

2. Pin Parvati Trek: Pin Parvati is one of those treks that should be placed in the Himachal travel packages of all the challenge seekers. This trek lasts for around 7 to 8 days and takes the hikers to a whopping elevation of about 17,450 feet above sea level. In the course of their trekking, the hikers pass through snowy landscapes, huge boulders, undulating meadows, and lofty mountain passes. The panoramic view offered to the trekkers at the summit is unmatched and too surreal to miss out on. Pin Parvati Trek is suitable for seasoned trekkers with excellent stamina and fitness level.

3. Kheerganga Trek: The Kheerganga Trek takes the trekkers to the shimmering, frothy-white Kheerganga river that falls from the lofty mountains. This trek is quite popular among trekking enthusiasts owing to its low difficulty level and a short length. Offering a 12 kilometers easy trail, this trek can be covered within 5 to 6 hours if the trekkers only take a few short breaks.

4. Triund Trek: Offering a spectacular panoramic view of the mighty Dhauladhar Ranges, Triund Trek is considered to be an amazing destination for experiencing solo trekking. Perched at a decent altitude of around 9,350 feet and offering an easy trail, this trek can be covered by people of all age groups and expertise levels. Triund Trek is generally done over a period of 2 days and the trekkers love to enjoy bonfires and night camping under the starry sky on the first night of the trek.

5. Beas Kund Trek: Lying in the lofty Dhauladhar Range, Beas Kund happens to be a wonderful spot with a deep historical significance. This scenic place is perched at an approximate altitude of around 12,139 feet and can be reached by covering an easy trek of 15 to 17 kilometers. The trails of the Beas Kund Trek are not very arduous and can be covered within a period of 2 days.
