Ladakh - Land of High Passes

High altitude mountain passes, Pangong Lake & ancient Buddhist monasteries

Duration ⏱️ 6 Days / 5 Nights
Traveler Rating 4.8 ★ (2.8K Reviews)

Destination Overview

Ladakh is a rust-colored desert mountain landscape in the high Himalayas. Experience crystal-clear high-altitude lakes, ancient Buddhist monasteries perched on cliff edges, double-humped camel safaris, and unforgettable mountain pass road trips.

Detailed Day-by-Day Itinerary

Day 1

Arrival in Leh & Acclimatization

Arrive at Kushok Bakula Rimpochee Airport, Leh. Transfer to hotel and spend the day relaxing to acclimatize to high altitude. Evening visit to Leh Palace and Shanti Stupa for panoramic sunset views.

Day 2

Leh Local Sightseeing & Sham Valley

Visit Hall of Fame, Magnetic Hill, Confluence of Indus and Zanskar rivers at Nimmu, and Gurudwara Pathar Sahib. Return to Leh for overnight stay.

Day 3

Leh to Nubra Valley via Khardung La Pass

Drive through Khardung La Pass (17,582 ft) - one of the world’s highest motorable roads. Arrive in Hunder, Nubra Valley. Enjoy double-humped camel safari among sand dunes.

Day 4

Nubra Valley to Pangong Tso Lake via Shyok

Drive along the picturesque Shyok River to the famous high-altitude Pangong Lake (13,862 ft). Marvel at the changing colors of the lake and stay overnight in luxury desert camps.

Day 5

Pangong Tso to Leh via Chang La Pass

Witness sunrise over Pangong Tso. Drive back to Leh via Chang La Pass. En route, visit Hemis Monastery and Thiksey Monastery.

Day 6

Departure from Leh

Transfer to Leh Airport with unforgettable memories of Ladakh.
