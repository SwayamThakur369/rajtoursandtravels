What is MICE, and how does it benefit my business?

MICE stands for Meetings, Incentives, Conferences, and Exhibitions. With the ever evolving industry trends and travel preferences, business and corporate organizations have started thinking beyond just leisure and conventional business trips. Therefore, MICE emerges as a strategic approach to corporate events that combines business objectives with engaging experiences. MICE events can enhance team collaboration, motivate employees, and create lasting memories.
